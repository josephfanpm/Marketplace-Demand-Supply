How to read this

The question

Fill rate is falling while demand grows. The default fix is "recruit more workers", but posted hours are lost for many reasons, and only some are a shortage.

The idea

Predict how well each market's shifts should fill from their traits alone (pay, posting time, approval, weekends, nights). Compare that with actual fill. Below expected means a market problem; low expected means a shift problem.

Who acts

Recruiting: markets below expected. Client success: hard-to-fill shifts. Operations: callouts. Account managers: spare capacity, so close leads.

Fill rate vs expected fill

Expected fill comes from shift traits only (pay vs local, posting lead time, approval, weekend, night, slots). Expected flat while actual falls → the market lost capacity, not the shifts.
